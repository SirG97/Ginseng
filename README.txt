GINSENG FIVE TREASURE — ONE-PAGE WEBSITE

1. Open index.html and replace every occurrence of:
   234XXXXXXXXXX
   with your WhatsApp business number, including country code and without + or spaces.

2. Upload index.html to your server/public_html/www root.

3. The page is intentionally self-contained: no framework, build process, or external assets are required.

4. Before publishing, replace the CSS product mockup with your actual product photo if you have one. Also update the ingredients and any claims so they exactly match your packaging and applicable regulations.

5. Online ordering can be added later by replacing the WhatsApp CTA with a checkout/order form and backend.

The layout was inspired by common high-converting patterns seen on Nigerian Ginseng Five Treasure product pages: prominent hero CTA, clear ingredient section, simple preparation steps, social-proof/benefit structure, and WhatsApp-first ordering. Claims have been kept conservative rather than copying medical or sexual claims from competitor pages.
